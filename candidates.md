# Candidates — Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist

Judge criteria, in order (RULES.txt): (1) on-subject one-line forecast for a
city that does not exist, absurd yet believable; (2) funnier, joke lands on
first read, deadpan twist; (3) more vivid, concrete image/number/detail;
(4) believable broadcast tone; (5) uses its 160 characters well.
Scores are 1–5 per criterion.

## Notes

- v1 (Mildford): "100% chance of weather" is a known joke shape; vivid detail
  is thin (no image beyond the numbers). Probe only.
- v2 (Snooze Falls): most vivid ("scattered showers of missed alarms"),
  but the joke is whimsical rather than deadpan; the city name does the
  setup, the forecast does the punchline.
- v3 (Mehville) — SHIPPED: the city name IS the joke (Mehville = a town
  that does not care), the 66/65 near-identical high/low is deadpan
  filler, and "a 100% chance of nobody caring" is the twist that lands on
  one read in a straight broadcast voice. Concrete numbers throughout,
  nothing explained, no cliché. Tie with v2 on total; wins the tie-break
  on criterion 2 (funnier on first read) and on the name carrying the joke.
- A5 loses on tone (reads like a joke, not a broadcast line); A8 loses on
  subject (breakups are not weather); A9/A10 have weak jokes.
